Copperline Press

Letterpress printing, one colour at a time

Two Heidelberg platens in a railway arch in Bristol. We print business cards, invitations and stationery on cotton stock heavy enough to hold an impression you can read with your thumb.

What we print

Cards, compliment slips, invitations and menus. Up to three colours, each one its own plate and its own pass through the press.

How it runs

Send artwork as vector outlines. We cut plates, make ready, and print a proof for you to approve before the run.

The studio

Arch 14, Lower Approach Road. Open Tuesday to Friday, and on Saturdays when the presses are quiet.

What it will cost

Prices are worked out in the studio's own price list, not in this page. Ask for a figure and you get today's rates.
